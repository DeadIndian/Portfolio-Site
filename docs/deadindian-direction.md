## Selected Direction And Earlier Alternatives

### A. An Evolving 3D Workshop — Selected

A personal space that develops as the story unfolds. A learning desk, old builds,
desktop experiments, shared tools, traces of community, and an unfinished JARVIS
workbench belong to one coherent environment.

Visitors move between authored camera compositions and inspect meaningful
objects. Warm personal details sit alongside mechanical and technical detail.
The presentation is a spatial memoir; professional information remains in the
existing desktop interface.

**Why it fits:** His recurring instinct is to make things his own and make them
useful. A world he gradually builds expresses that directly. Objects need
specific stories and artifacts so the result feels like his space.

### B. A Journey Map With Connected 3D Dioramas — Earlier Alternative

An explorable map connects places representing learning, Linux, contributions,
his people, and the future. Each stop opens a compact scene and a real account.

**Why it fits:** It carries the sense of progression associated with gaming and
adventure. The map should follow his story, with direct chapter access and
meaningful interaction rather than invented levels, scores, or chores.

### C. A Personal Archive / Time Capsule — Earlier Alternative

An intimate collection of old screenshots, handmade projects, patches, notes,
and present-day experiments. Use a detailed 3D artifact table or display space,
with readable accounts alongside the objects.

**Why it fits:** Actual evidence of change makes the journey personal and gives
early work equal emotional importance to more technically ambitious work.
Dates and personal artifacts must come from available records or the owner.

### D. A Cinematic Personal Documentary — Earlier Alternative

A directed scroll experience built from original 3D still-life scenes, strong
typography, real screenshots, and short first-person accounts. Chapter changes
provide the visual drama. Sound or recorded narration can be considered later
if the owner wants them; the experience works through visuals and text.

**Why it fits:** It prioritizes an intentional emotional sequence and can borrow
cinematic techniques without depending on anime franchises.

**Decision:** A is the selected primary direction. Real screenshots, early builds,
notes, and patches can furnish the workshop with personal history. The other
directions remain here as a record of the earlier discussion.
